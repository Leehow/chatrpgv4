# 10 — Narration contract

The presentation graph composes voice, scene tone, NPC voice, pacing, sensory policy, disclosure policy, dialogue policy, content boundaries, and examples.

Narration must acknowledge the player's action, describe committed consequences, preserve uncertainty, and leave valid affordances. It must not decide player-character interiority, repeat input as filler, expose Keeper-only claims, name unidentified entities, explain every detail, or add uncommitted hard facts.

Re-rendering a commit creates another render revision and never another world commit. Secret leakage and uncommitted facts are errors; repetitive phrasing may be a warning eligible for local repair.
