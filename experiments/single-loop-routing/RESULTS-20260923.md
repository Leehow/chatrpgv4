## Misses and risks recorded (do not tune these away by hand)

- **Scene craft is not host-issuable.** Who meets the investigator at the morgue and that it costs a Persuade are not facts the kernel or the Mod states; Jev correctly says `ask_llm`. The design's saving depends on how much of a scene's demands are data (pending contacts, declared gatekeeper checks) rather than Keeper improvisation. This is the main open question for SL-02.
- **`now`/`later` for a person is ~50/50** even with the morgue material: staging the gatekeeper is "part of the declared action" only in the Keeper's reading.
- **Jev's absolute confidence is low on multi-way questions**; the margin gate is a prototype parameter, not a product threshold. Every distribution is in the traces.
- **The read step is the product prescreen** (7 Jev calls on a cold locate, 2–3 warm, 0.9–2.5 s). In the loop it is the dominant Jev cost; its reuse cache is what makes runs 2–3 cheap.
- **Ordinary checks were never routed by Jev on this turn**: the ordinary-check candidate sat at ≤ 0.22 `now` because the input declares no check; the binder (route/profile) was therefore not exercised here. It is exercised in `loop.test.mjs` with a stub.
- Not tested: a turn whose declared action is itself a check ("我搜这间屋子"), pending player choices, combat/chase sessions, cancellation mid-run, and the LLM's context projection size (recorded as `context_projection_bytes` only).
