## Misses and risks recorded (do not tune these away by hand)

- **Scene craft is not host-issuable.** Who meets the investigator at the morgue and that it costs a Persuade are not facts the kernel or the Mod states; Jev correctly says `ask_llm`. The design's saving depends on how much of a scene's demands are data (pending contacts, declared gatekeeper checks) rather than Keeper improvisation. This is the main open question for SL-02.
- **`now`/`later` for a person is ~50/50** even with the morgue material: staging the gatekeeper is "part of the declared action" only in the Keeper's reading.
- **Jev's absolute confidence is low on multi-way questions**; the margin gate is a prototype parameter, not a product threshold. Every distribution is in the traces.
- **The read step is the product prescreen** (7 Jev calls on a cold locate, 2–3 warm, 0.9–2.5 s). In the loop it is the dominant Jev cost; its reuse cache is what makes runs 2–3 cheap.
- **Ordinary checks were never routed by Jev on this turn**: the ordinary-check candidate sat at ≤ 0.22 `now` because the input declares no check; the binder (route/profile) was therefore not exercised here. It is exercised in `loop.test.mjs` with a stub.
- Not tested: a turn whose declared action is itself a check ("我搜这间屋子"), pending player choices, combat/chase sessions, cancellation mid-run, and the LLM's context projection size (recorded as `context_projection_bytes` only).

# SL-02 — the product driver (pre-registration written 2026-09-23, before the scored runs)

Everything above this line is the prototype's record and is unchanged. This section belongs to SL-02 of
`docs/specs/pi-native-single-loop.md`. Its scored results are appended below the pre-registration after the runs.

## Instrument

`run.mjs --llm replay` now runs `product-entry.ts`, which exercises the **product driver**, not the prototype's
`runTurn`. The pieces are:

- A real Pi `AgentSession` from the vendored build (`build/node_modules`), with `PI_COC_LOOP_ENGINE=hybrid-v1` and
  the product's `createHybridEngine`. That engine brings the step policy, the read step with the product
  prescreen, the host-issued candidates, Jev decisions, clerk writes through the kernel extension's canonical
  gateway, and the `coc-clerk` projection.
- The kernel, onboarding, module, memory and table extensions, over the emitted kernel on a disposable copy of the
  fixture, reset to the fixture's pre-turn commit. Jev is live, with the key read from the App's vault. The Mods
  extension is not loaded: neither fixture has queued Mod work at its pre-turn commit (`mods.queued` is empty for
  both, checked 2026-09-23). The clerk's Mod hooks are asserted at the extension seam instead
  (`single-loop-domain-policy.test.mjs`).
- No language model is called. The Keeper's provider is a replay:
  - each adjudicate step answers with the live Keeper's next recorded message, minus the calls the run already
    carried out (read from the Keeper verbs that succeeded this run);
  - a bind step answers with the live call that binds the chosen operation (the effect with the same key, or the
    resolve with the same decision and actor or target), and if none exists it answers like an adjudicate step;
  - the compose, or a step with no recorded call left, answers with the live turn's accepted delivery. That is its
    last recorded `narrate`/`ask` that the live kernel took, or else the delivered narration from the fixture's own
    turn record at `commit_after`.
  - A recorded call the live kernel refused is not replayed. In the fight round that call is the first `ask`
    (`options` carried `none`, which the kernel refused as `invalid_params`). The replay measures the loop, not
    that Keeper slip.
- The §32 review lane is a replay too. It answers each review with the live table's recorded verdict for the same
  verb, in order (`--admission lane`, the product default). `--admission jev` puts §32.10's typed reviewer first,
  live, and uses the replayed lane only as its fallback.

## Instrument-development runs (before this registration; not scored)

These runs were made while the instrument and the engine were being built. They are listed because they came
before the registration.

  prototype's summary shape and preview.
