## Read phase: detail

For a host-issued source_unit, inspect every assigned task.pages original page and prepare a compact additive reference delta for that indexed range. This is background source work, not a chosen player action. Preserve identities, conditions, clues and links in the range; keep partly described entities unready and retain outside-range dependencies rather than inventing them or expanding into the whole book. An empty or copyright-only unit may add no nodes but still requires coverage review of all assigned pages. Character-creation advice stays advisory even in these reference records.

For purpose detail, task.focus and task.question bound both extraction and review. An empty question means prepare the focused entity for its current use and its necessary dependencies, not every chapter, investigation branch or resident associated with that place. For a walk or observation, prepare what can be perceived on that chosen route and the source-authored conditions of the observation; do not add every building interior, resident dossier or later encounter merely because it is mentioned nearby. Keep any background or restriction necessary to interpret the current evidence correctly. When task.material is map, use native PDF info, contents and bookmarks first; if task.pages names candidate physical pages, reopen those exact pages before extracting; otherwise use a navigation-only overview and then reopen each nominated physical page with pages. Extract only the source-backed map/handout asset needed now, with multiple independently revealable semantic regions, place correspondence and annotation safety. Only pages actually viewed may appear in source_refs, image_sources or map_regions; if the full geography cannot be independently segmented, keep the result uncertain and do not widen the crop. Keep the tool-enabled read/write/edit/bash workflow and never use OCR or a PDF parser. Pages viewed for navigation or context are evidence access, never an instruction to extract everything on them. A map request needs its authored geography, reveal safety and necessary map/text connections; a map label alone does not require preparing that person's dossier or every later encounter there. Keep unrelated destinations thin and outside ready_nodes. Only add ready nodes that this use actually needs. Source facts that change the current interaction, access, safety or meaning remain necessary even when they occur on another page.

For purpose detail, finish with submit_reading as your sole final tool call: pass a small draft directly or omit it if the full draft is already written. A failed check gives concrete findings to repair in this same session. A passing check ends the phase; do not spend another model response on a closing explanation. The host still independently reviews and publishes the delta.
