## Read phase: opening

For purpose opening, prepare the earliest source-supported conversation or meaningful player decision. A genuine source-authored telephone exchange can be playable without preparing the later physical encounter or fight. Keep static recaps distinct from interactive contact, and preserve which letter, call or witness supplies each lead. Stop at that first playable interaction, not the first combat or the whole first chapter. Prepare that interaction and the source facts that govern it, including current participants and distant conditions. Preserve the selected entrance identity from task.focus and known_nodes. If task.opening_scope is first_interaction, set interaction_scene to the exact ready scene node id where that interaction occurs. An introductory letter, radiogram, creation meeting or travel montage is insufficient when it merely hands off to the actual first encounter. In that case prepare both the selected entry and the first interaction, connect them by the book's route or progression, and keep intermediate transit nodes thin. Otherwise interaction_scene is the entry itself. No other scene belongs in ready_nodes. Do not skip a necessary intervening interaction or prepare later optional encounters. For an older task without this scope, exactly the selected entry scene is ready. Include the people actually present, available clues and handouts, applicable rules and necessary participant parameters for each ready scene; these also belong in ready_nodes unless already ready. An absent live-state input or discretionary presentation choice remains explicit and does not justify endless source searching. Handout crops needed only for later research remain deferred. Stop once the first interaction and its source dependencies are prepared; use submit_reading alone with the small draft when it fits, without a closing reply or an extra chapter sweep.

An introduction may offer optional research, a packet of historical articles or alternative backstory handouts before arrival. Unless the current task requests that investigation, keep these as thin sourced leads outside ready_nodes. Prepare the entry's essential hook and the chosen first interaction; do not transcribe every optional pre-arrival research branch. Retain source locations so the actual player demand can prepare them later. The reviewer checks this scope and must not demand those optional branches as missing current encounter material.
