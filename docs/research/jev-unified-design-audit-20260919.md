## Primary comparisons consulted

- TypeSafe [`patterns/fan-out`](https://docs.typesafe.ai/patterns/fan-out): parallel independent questions, host-routed answers.
- TypeSafe [`cookbooks/function_calling`](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/function_calling): typed closed arguments, no free numeric or text generation.
- TypeSafe [`cookbooks/autoformat`](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/autoformat): preserve original text and classify; do not copy its whitespace normalization into the exact-source contract.
- TypeSafe [`cookbooks/entity_alignment`](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/entity_alignment): uncertain pair links, not automatic world truth.
- TypeSafe [`cookbooks/semantic_find`](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/semantic_find): support plus ranking; Choice up to 255 options.
- Anthropic [`building-effective-agents`](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents): feedback loops and stop conditions.
- Microsoft Azure [`event-sourcing`](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/patterns/event-sourcing): rebuildable projections and idempotence, not a new database.
- W3C [annotation text-position selector](https://www.w3.org/TR/annotation-model/#text-position-selector) and [RFC 6901](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6901): locator prior art, noted for the deliberate UTF-16 versus normalized code-point difference.

Provider examples are not product proof and no long quotes or provider guarantees are reproduced.
