# Speech recovery: focused specification

## Purpose and boundary

Produce natural, situated NPC speech while preserving source truth, player choice, persistent identity, listener identity, and a single-pass turn. This slice adds no new game authority, no duplicate state, no source rewrite, no post-prose semantic judge, and no silent migration or packaging of old cards. Fresh campaigns and freshly generated cards are the acceptance surface; existing evidence and source-authored or established cards remain intact.

The existing interfaces remain the interfaces: actual apply/resolve admission and tool-result feedback; `voice.job →` background Tool-Pi author `→ review → voice.submit`; the existing capsule/context supplied to `before_provider_request`; and real driver single-player turns. The voice packet is bounded and source-bound. It carries the relevant people, known facts, receipts, listener, source voice, secrets, current situation, recent said lines, language and existing card context. It must not become a second fact store or a prose-derived state channel.

## Admission and action continuity

The five existing authorization verdicts remain unchanged. A refusal may carry the optional recovery enum `correct_proposal`, but only when the player already chose an act and the Keeper's proposed arguments misrepresent that act (an argument mismatch, not permission to add an act). This is not automatic authorization: it is a host-owned correction allowance, at most one per active player turn; that allowance is not durable across process restart. The corrected proposal is reviewed afresh. Repeating the originally rejected call remains rejected; there is no retry loop. If the player has not chosen the missing act, the original refusal stands. The current declared act is reconciled forward to actual receipts without retconning prior deliveries; a previously delivered canon result may still be landed forward when the state requires it. Keep the declared act distinct from the receipts actually observed.

Preserve atomic batches and all existing cash, target, destination, method, quantity, duration and scope restrictions. A correction must not add a route, target, method, price, promise, object, or commitment. A correctable refusal is labeled separately in retained context so it does not withdraw the act the player did choose. Existing handover consent metadata remains a ground stated by the Keeper: `given` is not a gesture or player utterance. Existing settled receipts remain authoritative. No reviewer may convert a proposal, rationale, source price, NPC demand, or offer into consent.

## Facts and objects

Use the existing capsule fields for people, known information and receipts as pre-draft anchors. Preserve the same identity through name or role changes. Distinguish what is carried, shown, handed over, declared, and settled. A declaration is not a settled fact merely because it appears in prose; a receipt is not a new invitation to narrate an unchosen act. Input metaphor or image is likewise not identity evidence: distinguish the input's figurative framing from the person, object, role, or identity established by source and receipts. Object state changes only through the existing define/object and queued identity/owed transactions. Do not infer a hidden object transition from a line of dialogue, duplicate state.

Source-consistent wording is required. A metaphor, image, atmosphere, occupational register, or card example may shape how a line sounds, but it is not automatically a literal object, event, habit, secret, relationship, or state. Do not turn descriptive source language into a newly asserted fact. Conversely, do not delete a source-established fact merely because it is not convenient for a short answer. The current listener and scene determine whether a known fact is spoken now.

## Authoring and review

Tool-Pi writes the prose artifact. Jev never generates text: it makes bounded semantic judgments only. The card packet is reviewed question by question. Each question asks one defect in the candidate example request/reply context against `state.source` and `state.candidate`; questions are not limited to an absent question in the current source. A high-confidence clean result across every check accepts; a high-confidence defect rejects; missing, uncertain, or unavailable evidence goes to the existing Luna semantic review. Jev checks run in the background with the bounded card-review contract: `Jev1.13.0`, clear `<=0.1`, defect `>=0.9`, 2,000 ms per review, 24,000 state bytes, and 12 independent defect questions. Unknown, incomplete, or unavailable Jev evidence falls back to `flapcode/gpt-6-luna low`. There is no key or new budget reset, and the existing single reviewed repair and cancellation/publication guards remain in force.

A card may represent flexible voice, not a compulsory task. The mask describes register, attitude, address, or word choice; it does not require a catchphrase, refusal, agreement, question, fixed topic, fixed agenda, sentence length, or rhythm. Ordinary greeting and courtesy remain possible. A relevant practical question may receive a longer or formal connected answer, direct answer, uncertainty, evasion, silence, protest, or refusal as the source and encounter warrant. A repeated topic is not itself a defect: review the actual present question and response. Do not impose mandatory brevity, courtesy, refusal, or a particular language branch. No hardcoded language keyword bans, regexes, wordlists, name inference, defaults, or executable semantic heuristics may decide these judgments.

Review must preserve the listener, established identity, source facts, secrets, agency, language and writing system. It must reject invented names, places, resources, authority, commitments, discoveries, or hidden facts; disclosed secrets; contradictions with the listener; and treating source imagery as literal spoken habit. It must also detect nonresponsive exchange, monotonous range, coarse conflict, fixed agenda, and mandated rhythm without confusing ordinary repetition with failure. A silent source remains silent only when the source explicitly establishes that the person does not speak; reserve, shyness, brevity, or a quiet voice is not enough.

## Models and authority

All prose, live authoring, and fallback review use the existing explicitly configured `flapcode/gpt-6-luna low` model. Bounded Jev judgments use `Jev1.13.0`. Astra and Grok are prohibited. Model output never changes authority by itself: admission authority remains in the existing admission rule, and publication remains behind review and cancellation guards. The host carries closed shapes and receipts; semantic meaning remains a direct source-grounded model judgment.

## Acceptance gates

Acceptance is honest and layered. Structural validation must confirm only the requested artifact shapes, required question keys, and existing interface compatibility. Focused public regressions must cover positive return/place behavior, negative unchosen extra cost/target/method behavior, the one-correction budget, no automatic authority, and atomic batches. Card review must cover clean, reject, uncertain, and cancellation paths; actual provider payloads must be checked; and a new real-driver table must exercise final-source versions. Distinguish code correctness, actual delivery, dialogue quality, and complete ending receipt. Do not claim success for a gate that was not run, and do not claim perfection from a passing structural check.
