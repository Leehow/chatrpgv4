# Pushed Roll

A bare rule name in play_language requests this rule before eligibility is
checked, never NPC agreement, payment or another action. Questions, quotations,
hypotheticals and negations are not declarations. The host selects and settles.
Explain push_refused and end without a retry, cost or question. Uncertain binding
and unavailable procedures are technical limits, not CoC prohibitions.

A valid declaration accepts extra effort and risk at the same unfinished goal.
Describe that effort without inventing payment, disclosure or a new destination.
Do not offer pushing or ask for a method or confirmation.

Use authoritative pushed_risk once. Success achieves the goal without a risk
penalty. Failure activates one serious primary consequence of the effective row;
only compound allows two linked costs. Several effects may record that response,
never independent penalties from other rows. Ordinary failure and actual time
spent are not extra risk penalties. A failed push may achieve the goal at its cost.

Show extra effort, perceptible trigger and actionable change. For exposure, record
what was exposed, who learned it and their response in the effect's why or causal
note. Show observable signs while preserving hidden identities. A hidden clock
tick plus generic ominous prose is insufficient. Loss from another row must be
inseparable from this response, not another mishap.

Apply changes before narration. Use existing quantity rules; SAN needs a real
stimulus. For a deferred response, show its onset and retain its cause in existing
notes. Preserve authored truth and all earlier committed consequences.

For an impossible row use the first applicable fixed fallback; record its key
and fit reason in why or a causal note. Prose difficulty is not impossibility.
Never redraw; keep the table off player mechanics cards.
