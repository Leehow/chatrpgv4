# Pushed Roll

A bare statement of this rule's name in play_language requests the rule, even
before any check. Recognize it before evaluating eligibility. It never authorizes
accepting an NPC offer, taking payment or another fictional action. The host
selects and settles it. Explain push_refused briefly in play_language and end the
turn without a retry, penalty or clarification. Unavailable judgment means an
uncertain binding; do not falsely claim the rules forbid retrying. Missing procedures are technical
limits, not CoC prohibitions. Questions, quotations, hypotheticals and negations
are not declarations.

An eligible declaration accepts extra effort and risk at the same unfinished
goal. Do not ask for a changed method, confirmation or another reply; do not offer
pushing automatically. Describe effort within that goal without inventing a new
voluntary payment, disclosure or destination.

Read authoritative pushed_risk; do not draw again. Success achieves the goal with
no failed-push penalty. On failure, treat the effective row as a constraint, not
just inspiration: realize one primary serious consequence of that type, tied to
the action and connected dangers. Do not add independent penalties from other
rows unless compound was drawn. Multiple apply effects may record one consequence;
they are not permission for multiple unrelated costs. Failure to achieve the goal
and genuinely spent time are not extra risk penalties. Achieving the goal at the
drawn cost is allowed.

Show a perceptible causal chain: the extra effort, what it triggers, and how that
changes the situation the player can act on. For exposure, privately establish
what became known, who learned it, and their concrete response in the existing
effect's why or causal note. Show observable signs of that response without naming
an undiscovered observer. A hidden clock tick plus generic ominous atmosphere is
insufficient. Do not also destroy equipment or permanently close a route merely
to intensify the failure; such losses need their drawn row or an inseparable part
of the same response, not a second independent mishap.

Apply actual injury, equipment, time, NPC or world changes before narrating them.
Use existing causal notes for later consequences; when a response is deferred,
show its perceptible onset now and preserve its cause for later play. Quantities
use existing rules; SAN requires a real stimulus. Preserve authored truth and
committed consequences; never undo an earlier penalty to fit these instructions.

For an impossible row use its first applicable fallback; record the effective key
and fit reason in the consequence's why or causal note. Prose difficulty is not
impossibility. Compound means one event with two linked costs, never another draw.
Keep the table off player mechanics cards.
