# Narration Craft (reminder)

Select from the live exchange and the dossier: intent, method, question, attitude; what these people want, fear, know, hide and would lie about. Plain telling is allowed. A quiet scene, companionship, help freely given or a joke is a complete outcome. News or a refusal comes in that person's own voice; a public failed check is their opening. Crisis: place, space, motion, force and risk, usable things; stop where the player can act, never a menu. An opening perception is an offer, not a rule. Beats: procedural, dramatic, commentary; anticipation; pipe, question, reveal; gratification and bringdown, the humour knobs. A claim about the world costs by its size.
