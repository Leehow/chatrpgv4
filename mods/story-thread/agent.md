# Story Thread

The capsule's `mods.thread` reorganises the module by what the story still needs, not by where the party stands. Each line is one authored conclusion the discovered clues have not yet closed: `missing` of `of` clues remain, and `minimum_routes` says how few the book needs. `here` lists this scene's clues with their gate and the book's own delivery words; `next` lists the scenes one move away that hold more, how many, the book's words on getting them, and whether the way is unlocked; `beyond` counts the rest; `fallback` is the book's own recovery when nothing on that line has been found yet.

It is where the story can still go; take a line when the fiction reaches it, not because a turn has passed. When the player's action reaches a clue in `here`, land it with `apply clue`. A row's `gate` names how the book delivers the clue and what check it asks for: named skill and difficulty mean that check as declared; `check required (skill unspecified)` means the delivery is a check but the projected skill is unnamed, so choose from available context/source and look up source only when needed; `check unspecified` means this projection has no named check, not that a real risk is free and not that you should add a roll. Use player intent and known rule/source detail. Ordinary uncontested actions and plainly given clues gain no check just because this field is unspecified. Costs only come from rules, source or investigator actions; if such a cost is established without a failing check, spend time, noise, a tool or help. When the player asks where to go or has nowhere to go, put the directions in `next` within reach through the fiction: an NPC's hint, a document, a road seen from the window; never a menu. When a line has no `here` and no `next`, that is what `fallback` is for.

`handed` clues are ones the book means to happen: what is plainly in the room, or what a present person says when asked. They are not routes and not choices to offer; the timing is yours, but do not lock them behind a check the book did not write. Critical conclusions come first. A line closing is not the end of the story: a quest that `can close` in obligations and an ending in `where.endings` say what remains to settle.

Continuity is not a quota: preserve `mods.thread.connections` for acquired clue-to-conclusion relations, but acquisition is not understanding. For cross-scene relationship evidence use `lookup` kind `continuity` with semantic-name query/anchors, or `recall` older deliveries when context is truncated. Explain public facts and let knowledgeable NPCs speak plainly; do not invent activity, deadlines, checks, costs, clues, or force interpretation.
