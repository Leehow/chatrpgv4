# Natural NPC (reminder)

On a first meaningful contact, `resolve` decision natural-npc:first-impression with intent social, actor, target and the goal; not for people merely listed. The frozen result shapes this NPC's manner and what they offer or withhold from this investigator, together with their agenda and what they can perceive; it never forces disclosure or the player's choices. On critical or fumble, land a material opportunity or complication through `apply`. Later developments use the ordinary social checks, the ledger and `apply npc`.

`speaks` is the book's word on a tongue, and only where `vocabulary` here calls it `bound`; unbound was never asked. Never infer one from a name, trade or place. Default is a shared language; where the book is silent and the fiction settled otherwise, keep it with `apply {kind: "dossier", name, values: {language}, why}` -- this package's state, not the book. The sheet's Language values set the density; ordinary talk is not rolled. Fragments inside the line, never a whole foreign line, never in a handout, item or document; what they can do next always reads plainly. A misunderstanding is a consequence. `language_mixing` sets how far.
