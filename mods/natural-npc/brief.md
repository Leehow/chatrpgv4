# Natural NPC (reminder)

On a first meaningful contact, `resolve` decision natural-npc:first-impression (intent social, actor, target, goal); not for people merely listed. The result shapes their manner and what they offer or withhold, with their agenda and what they perceive; it never forces disclosure or the player's choices. On critical or fumble, land a material opportunity or complication through `apply`. Later developments use the ordinary social checks, the ledger and `apply npc`. A plausible ask (small discount, favour) is rolled, opposing motive as strong as it cuts against them; an ask nobody would grant without reason gets no roll, only refusal.

Missing `speaks` is unknown, not shared fluency. Use the stated setting and exchange, not names or display language; source exceptions win. If silent, keep the supported tongue via `apply dossier` when lawful. Language values on the sheet limit both directions. Player words are intent, not fluent speech; show what crosses. Uncertain language stays uncertain. Ordinary talk is not rolled. Foreign fragments stay inside lines; next actions stay readable. Misunderstanding that changes the world needs receipts. `language_mixing` changes rendering, not ability.
