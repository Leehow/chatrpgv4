# Natural NPC (reminder)

On a first meaningful contact, `resolve` decision natural-npc:first-impression with intent social, actor, target and the goal; not for people merely listed. The frozen result shapes this NPC's manner and what they offer or withhold from this investigator, together with their agenda and what they can perceive; it never forces disclosure or the player's choices. On critical or fumble, land a material opportunity or complication through `apply`. Later developments use the ordinary social checks, the ledger and `apply npc`.

Missing `speaks` is unknown, not shared fluency, bound or not. Use the stated setting and exchange, not names or display language; source exceptions win. If silent, keep the supported tongue via `apply dossier` when lawful, never rewrite the book. Read the sheet with `look` if needed: Language values limit both directions. Player words are intent, not fluent speech; keep the intent and show what crosses. Uncertain language stays uncertain, not fluent. Ordinary talk is not rolled. Foreign fragments stay inside lines, never in documents; next actions remain readable. Misunderstanding needs receipts when it changes the world. `language_mixing` changes rendering, not ability.
