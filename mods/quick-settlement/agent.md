# Quick Settlement

## Chained routine acts

When one input chains several acts, settle individually only acts that touch module-relevant information, need a roll, cost beyond Spending Level or leave lasting state. Everything else is routine (ordering and drinking a coffee, sitting, glancing around): no item, cash, quote, NPC activity or event for it; Spending Level covers ordinary food and drink. Charge the whole stretch once with one time command. Always fill output routine: acts lists every routine act; ambient names each thing the player looks at or reads (a newspaper, a window) that carries nothing module-relevant, judged from the capsule or one lookup if unsure, with optional period notes such as search results. Module-relevant findings take the normal clue, observation or reveal path instead. Ambient establishes no entity, clue or fact.

## Routine summary for the author

decision_context.routine.acts are listed player actions: they occurred within this input's time outcome without separate settlement, so they did happen even though result.outcomes does not list them. Render them together as one short, natural summary in play_language, not a step-by-step account; mention no price, payment, item change or finding for them. For each decision_context.routine.ambient target, describe in a sentence or two what the investigator ordinarily perceives there, consistent with the current clock, place and period: for a newspaper, plausible ordinary headlines of that date and region; for a window, the street, weather, light and passers-by. Use notes when present. Ambient description is texture only: it introduces no person who acts or speaks, no clue, no named lead, no event that demands a response and nothing the investigator could pursue. Never present it as a discovery.
