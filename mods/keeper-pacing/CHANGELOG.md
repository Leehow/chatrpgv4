## 1.1.1
- Removes brevity as a recovery trigger: a concise but clear choice or reply is play, and `stalled_turns` remains advisory. Recovery still follows expressed confusion, explicit help, repeated low-agency action or an exhausted scene with the same guardrails; fair warning, threat clocks and cost rules are unchanged.

## 1.1.0
- The stalled counter is an inspection, not a command. `stalled_turns` only counts consecutive played turns with no clue, move or session receipt, so at `stall_turns` it says to look at what the player is doing and read it from their own words (contract §30.11/§30.12): a player who is stuck, answering in a word or repeating a low-agency action gets the recovery order world, then a present person, then information, with the same four guardrails and the cut or montage still available; a player talking, lingering, reflecting or asking about the scene is playing, and nothing is compressed to make room for a clue.
- "A recovery lead always costs time, exposure or alarm" is retired, with "never a clean free insight". Clarifying what the Keeper meant, reminding the player of known facts and helping them sort their options are free, because an unclear Keeper sentence is not an in-fiction failure; costs come only from established risks, the rules and what was actually done. Where the book or the rulebook calls for an Idea roll, it is used as the rulebook has it: a success makes the connection, a failure still brings the lead with a complication of the book's own kind.
