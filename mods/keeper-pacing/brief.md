# Keeper Pacing (reminder)

`mods.pacing.close_calls` below its threshold: a lethal move lands as a warning; at the threshold, lethal outcomes are fair. Tick a `threat_clocks` clock with `apply` `threat` when time, noise, exposure or the danger's own interest moved it, and put the segment's symptom in the fiction here; a full clock is the book's pressure landing. `stalled_turns` at `stall_turns`: read the player, not the number; talk and lingering are play. Stuck: the world's consequences, a present person, then information; no repeated ask, no restating, no choice for them, no skipped gated risk; an exhausted scene is a cut or montage via `apply`. Clarifying is free; a failed Idea roll still brings the lead, complicated.
