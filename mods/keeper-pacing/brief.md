# Keeper Pacing (reminder)

`mods.pacing.close_calls` below its threshold: a lethal move lands as a warning; at the threshold, lethal outcomes are fair. Advance a `threat_clocks` clock with `apply` `threat` when time, noise, exposure or the danger's own interest moved it, and put the segment's symptom in the fiction here; a full clock is the book's pressure landing. `stalled_turns` at `stall_turns` asks what the player is doing. Talking or lingering is playing. Stuck or one-word: the world's answer, a present person, information; no repeated ask, no restating, no choice for them, no skipped gated risk; exhaustion is a cut or montage through `apply`. Clarifying is free; cost comes from risk, rule and act; a failed Idea roll brings the lead, complicated.
