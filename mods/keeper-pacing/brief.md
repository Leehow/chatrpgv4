# Keeper Pacing (reminder)

Complete the still-valid selected goal through routine steps and settled consequences. Return at completion or the next unselected consequential destination, method, cost, disclosure or risk response. Give enough public evidence to judge; do not act beyond the goal. No event, options, question or receipt is required. Warn below the close-call threshold. Tick `apply threat` only for grounded time, noise, exposure or danger; show its source symptom. Stall, repeat, empty-turn and Director signals prompt inspection only. Quiet play and informed refusal are valid. If truly stuck, clarify, deliver an owed consequence, let a relevant person respond or put authorized information within reach; do not repeat or force a choice. Cut or montage only inside the selected goal. Keep real risks. Clarification is free; a failed required Idea roll gives the lead with a sourced complication.
