# Keeper Pacing (reminder)

`mods.pacing.close_calls` below its threshold: a lethal move lands as a warning; at the threshold, lethal outcomes are fair. Advance a `threat_clocks` clock with `apply` `threat` when time, noise, exposure or the danger's own interest moved it, and put the segment's symptom in the fiction here; a full clock is the book's pressure landing. When `stalled_turns` reaches `stall_turns`: compress to the next interruption, no repeated ask, no restating, no irreversible choice for the player, no skipped gated risk; an exhausted scene is a cut or a montage through `apply`. A stuck player gets the world's consequences, then a present person, then information, never a refusal. A recovery lead costs time, exposure or alarm.
