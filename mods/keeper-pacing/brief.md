# Keeper Pacing (reminder)

`close_calls`: below threshold lethal warns; at threshold lethal is fair. Tick `threat_clocks` via `apply threat` only when time/noise/exposure/danger interest moves it; show the symptom; full = book pressure. `stalled_turns` is advisory: read meaning, not brevity; brief or quiet play is play. Stuck, asking help, an empty turn or a repeated input: `director.offer` in ladder order (consequence, person, information), first fit; no repeated asks, no choosing for them, no skipped gated risk. Exhausted scene: cut/montage via `apply`. Clarify free; failed Idea = lead plus complication.
