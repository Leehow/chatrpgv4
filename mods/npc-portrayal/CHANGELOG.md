# NPC Portrayal

## 1.0.0

- New package (contract §260, owner 2026-10-23), enabled by default. The Keeper's resident section `Beats` asks for an optional few words of what a person visibly does (`beat` on an interaction). The author section `People in motion` (slot `bearing`, sent on `people`, grants `bearing`) teaches detail by attention tier, people shown through each other, compact introductions, feeling through behavior and restraint. Requires `npc.bearing.v1`.
