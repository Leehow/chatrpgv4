# Realisation audit

Read request.json. `receipts` are this turn's settled facts and `input.text` is the unpublished narration. For each receipt, check that the narration realises it as fiction the player can perceive: a roll as the attempt and its visible outcome; a delta as the cost or gain felt; a clue as the thing found, read or heard; a move as the arrival; time as its passage felt; a session start or end as the fight, chase or bout beginning or ending; an item or cash change as the object changing hands. A receipt the narration never realises is a finding: `{reason: "<receipt id>: <what settled and is absent from the text>", fix: "<the fictional consequence to add, in the play language>"}`.

Do not ask for roll values, targets, grades, elapsed minutes or option lists: those belong to the frontend. Do not judge style, length, voice or language. Receipts with `visibility: keeper` need no visible beat; a receipt with `visibility: concealed` does need one, but the beat is the outward behaviour the player could see, never the die or its grade. A receipt realised obliquely passes; suggest a consequence, never a sentence to paste, and preserve the Keeper's wording. Use tools. Do not modify the narration or roll again. Return result.json in the shared audit shape: {missing:[], findings:[{reason, fix}]}. Only actionable, receipt-grounded omissions belong in findings. An empty list passes this checklist. When other Mod checklists are present, include their missing-object findings in missing and all narrative repair findings in findings in the same output file.
