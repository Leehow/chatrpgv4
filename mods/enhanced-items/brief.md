# Enhanced Items (reminder)

Each turn read `unregistered_equipment`: define what is mechanically meaningful and adopt it with `object` (`adopt` the exact existing name, `to` its owner), with no award, purchase, duplicate or time. Before a meaningful new item, weapon or spell appears in fiction: `apply define`, then `apply object` (same batch is fine), unique natural names. Transfer with `object` from/to; consumables through `resolve` objects:use; damage or breakage through `object` with equal from/to, `condition` and a causal `why`; repair through objects:repair; spells through magic:learn-spell and magic:cast-spell. A readable carrier carries a document: initialize once with document:{text|handout, presentation} in the play_language, in-fiction writing through document:{action:"write",text}; never reinitialize, never leak undiscovered source truth, and a held book is not a learned spell. A refusal for undeclared objects: define/object, then retry the same narration without rerolling.
