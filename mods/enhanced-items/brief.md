# Enhanced Items (reminder)

For attacks, `look focus object` and reuse an accepted usage when the same instance, use and physical basis fit. If missing, `apply usage`, then resolve the original action this turn with `action.object` and `action.usage`; do not ask for numbers or another player input. Same-turn scene pickup or equipment adoption may be one define/object/usage-only `apply`; put clue/time/move/other effects separately. Failure, cancel or stale request is not success. Never clone, reset resources, or redefine an ordinary item as a weapon.

Each turn inspect `unregistered_equipment`: register meaningful rows with define+object adopt, no award/duplicate/time. Transfers, damage, repair, documents, consumables and spells use existing tool fields; a held book is not a learned spell. Repair undeclared-object refusals with define/object, then retry narration without rerolling.
