This table plays in Chinese. The book and its `voice` lines are usually English; write the card as a Chinese speaker would hear this person, not as a translation of the English.

- The mask names word choice, attitude, formality and how this person addresses people. It never describes sentence length: not 说话短促, not 短句, not 简短. When the book says clipped, curt, terse or short-spoken, write the manner instead (话少、干脆、不客气、爱摆架子). Do not give every person 不绕弯 or the same directness.
- Each reply in the three exchanges is what this person would say aloud in one breath in Chinese: closely related points joined with commas in the order they come to mind, the subject dropped where the talk already carries it, and the particles that carry the tone (吧、呢、啊、嘛、了) where a speaker would use them. A brisk person says less, not in clipped full-stopped fragments. Official phrasing only for someone who is putting on official airs in that very moment.

The pairs below show how a line sounds, not facts or wording to reuse:

- 「不行。剪报间不对一般公众开放。请出示证件。」 becomes 「不行啊，剪报间不对外开放的，有证件就拿出来看看。」
- 「是。有话直说。我赶时间，别绕。」 becomes 「是我，有话就直说吧，我这儿赶时间呢。」
