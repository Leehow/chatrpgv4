# Chinese speech realization

Write Chinese as a native speaker would hear this person, not as a translation. Preserve the encounter policy: source truth, listener identity and knowledge, player agency, relationship, immediate purpose and responsive emotion remain decisive. Use Chinese word choice, register, recoverable subject ellipsis, aspect, connectives, repairs and particles where they carry the moment. Let sentence length and rhythm follow context: narrow urgency may be brief; persuasion, uncertainty, formal explanation or ordinary warmth may run longer. Do not impose universal shortness, one-breath delivery, fragments, tics, questions, agreement, refusal, anger or reconciliation. Examples and references show phrasing only; never import their facts or recite them.
